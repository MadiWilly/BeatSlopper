BeatSlopper · Round 3 · Direction A · Studio Flat

Rationale. This direction optimises for readability and room: three flat greys instead of shadows, so every control gets its contrast back; one view owns the whole width at a time (PATTERN, MIXER, SONG), and the selected track's sound lives in a bottom Devices strip that reads like Ableton Live's Device View, next to a Clip tab that holds the piano roll and a Keyboard tab. The tracks read like Live's Session mixer (coloured title bar, activator / solo / arm, sends, pan, tall fader with the meter beside it) sitting over FL's channel-rack step grid, and the left column is a Live-style browser (search on top, categories as a tree, the preset list with a preview-on-select cue) that also holds the project's recent files. Every scrollable area shows a 10 px scrollbar, every hit target is at least 28 px, labels never go under 11 px. What it gives up: the neumorphic warmth and depth of "Soft Console", the everything-on-one-page overview (you tab between PATTERN / MIXER / SONG and the bottom area is shared by Clip, Devices and Keyboard), and the right inspector of the original brief (the Devices strip is the inspector, so one place edits the track's sound). Ideas are taken from Live's layout and hierarchy only: no assets, no exact colours.

3 · Main window 1280 × 800 · PATTERN view

Top bar (transport + global), browser column, Live-style track strips over the step grid (drum rows with VOL / TUNE and M / S, 16 steps per bar with accents and the playhead, the selected track's note lane), the Devices strip collapsed to one row of the selected track's chain. 4 · PIANO ROLL (bottom area · CLIP tab, enlarged)

The split is dragged up: key column with three octaves visible, notes as bars (velocity = opacity, the lane below), ghost notes of the other tracks in their colours, playhead, zoom and snap, both scrollbars.

5 · MIXER view

FL-style: source strips (5 drums + tracks) with their insert-number box, 16 numbered inserts with name, colour, three slot pills, EQ3 knobs, DLY / REV sends, ROUTE, fader + meter + gain-reduction meter, M / S; MASTER pinned right with the limiter. Scrolls horizontally with a visible bar.

6 · SYNTH & FX (DEVICES tab, enlarged) with the preset browser

The browser column filtered to Lofi Keys with "Dusty Rhodes" selected and MODIFIED; the Devices chain: SYNTH (osc 1 / 2, mod, mode, filter + filter ADSR, amp ADSR, LFO, glide, pitch env, velocity) → DRIVE → LO-FI → CHORUS → SIDECHAIN → SENDS, then the global DELAY, REVERB (MORE), MASTER EQ (pop-out) and LIMITER. Scrolls horizontally.

7 · SONG view (+ KEYBOARD tab)

Chain editor with repeats, add / remove / move, the resulting timeline; PROJECT (new / open / save / save as / export WAV with repeats, recent) and VISUALIZER (scope / bars / radial, FULL). The bottom area shows the on-screen keyboard.

8 · Narrow 850 × 600 (the 150 % case)

Browser collapsed to a 36 px rail, the top bar drops its wordmark and MIDI port name, the tab strip compacts, strips narrow to 64 px and scroll, cells shrink to 28 × 26, the bottom area is collapsed to its tab bar. Nothing is cut off; each overflowing area shows its scrollbar.
