BeatSlopper · Design B · "Deck"

Rationale. Deck optimises for the FL Studio mental model done with discipline: a browser column on the left (presets by category, project, recent), the CHANNEL RACK as its own window (mute LED, pan / vol, insert number, coloured name button, sixteen step buttons in beat groups), the PIANO ROLL as the main window with FL's key column, velocity lane and ghost channels, and the MIXER as a bottom dock whose numbered inserts run left to right with the selected insert's slots enlarged in a side column. Everything is flat: four panel tones and 1 px borders carry depth, one amber accent carries state, the eight track colours carry identity, and every dock draws its scrollbar at all times. It gives up the single-page "everything visible" layout of Soft Console (synth, song and browser now live behind F-keys as windows), gives up soft shadows and pill shapes entirely, and asks the user to learn the F5..F9 view switcher; in return every view gets the full width, nothing is ever cut off at 850 logical points, and density comes from 28 px rows rather than from shrinking type.

3 · Main window · 1280 × 800 · browser + channel rack + piano roll

Mixer F6
Step 9 · click toggles · Shift click accents

Toolbar in FL order: project · undo/redo · transport + Pattern|Song · BPM (− value + TAP) · swing · position · master · hint panel · MIDI · views F5..F9 · theme. FOLLOW is global and lives on the rack. The rack is 664 wide by default (steps 24 px, 2 px gaps, 6 px between beats); drag the splitter to trade width with the roll.

4 · Same frame · MIXER drawer pulled up (F6)

The drawer pushes the workspace up (it can also float over it); the rack and roll keep their headers and show their scrollbars with shorter thumbs. Inserts 1..16 left to right, master pinned right, the selected insert (1 · Keys) enlarged in the side column with its three slots, EQ3, sends and route.

5 · SYNTH & FX workspace (F7) with the preset browser beside it

The rack collapses to its header (click the chevron or press F5 to bring it back). Browser shows the PRESETS section expanded: search, category chips, list with vinyl / drone tags, current preset with the MODIFIED marker and prev / next (Ctrl+PgUp / PgDn).

6 · SONG workspace (F8) · project section in the browser · visualizer window

Song chain as a timeline strip (blocks proportional to bars) plus the entry table with repeats and move buttons. PROJECT (new / open / save / save as / export WAV with repeats) and RECENT live in the browser column, like FL's "Current project" and "Recent files".

7 · Narrow · 850 × 600 logical (1280 physical at 150 %) · nothing cut off

Browser collapses to a 28 px rail, the rack drops its knob columns (values move to the tooltip) and scrolls its steps horizontally with the scrollbar drawn, the main window becomes a tab strip (PIANO ROLL | MIXER | SYNTH & FX | SONG), the mixer and keyboard collapse to bars. Minimum size 840 × 600.
